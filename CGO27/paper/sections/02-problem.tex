\section{Problem and Model}
Let a selected compiler system be a tuple $S=(L,P,B,C)$ of language components, ordered passes, backend components, and their contracts. Let $x_i$ be the artifact at boundary $i$. A structural verifier establishes $V_{str}(x_i)$. The selected system additionally relies on semantic facts $F$, such as producer identity, verified stack effects, shape/layout consistency, or backend capability support.

Each fact $f\in F$ has a state
\[
\sigma_i(f)\in\{\mathsf{valid},\mathsf{invalid},\mathsf{unknown}\}.
\]
A transformation $t$ declares an effect contract
\[
E_t=(R_t,G_t,P_t,I_t),
\]
where $R_t$ are required facts, $G_t$ generated facts, $P_t$ preserved facts, and $I_t$ invalidated facts. Every registered verifiable fact has at most one canonical verifier owner $owner(f)$ and one executable route $route(f)$. The contract also defines the first eligible boundary $due(f,i)$ after invalidation at boundary $i$.

Invalidating $f$ creates an obligation
\[
o=(f,owner(f),route(f),due(f,i),\mathsf{pending}).
\]
A successful verifier changes the fact to $\mathsf{valid}$ and the obligation to $\mathsf{discharged}$; a failed verifier rejects compilation. Missing routes, conflicting owners, and attempts to cross a due boundary with a pending obligation are errors.

The key distinction is
\[
V_{str}(x_i) \centernot\implies Valid(S,x_i).
\]
For the motivating intrinsic $q$, the opcode and operand schema can satisfy $V_{str}(x_i)$ while $k_q\notin K_B$, where $K_B$ is the capability set of the selected backend. The missing information is a relation between a structurally legal artifact and the selected composition.

\paragraph{Demand is not obligation.}
A demand-driven analysis manager may recompute an invalidated fact if a later consumer asks for it. Let $D_i$ be the facts demanded before boundary $i$. If $f\notin D_i$, lazy recomputation performs no work even when $\sigma_i(f)=\mathsf{invalid}$. By contrast, obligation discharge depends on $due(f,i)$, not on $D_i$. This distinction matters precisely when the backend or runtime consumes the artifact without consulting the analysis cache.

\paragraph{Assumptions.}
Our guarantee is relative to: (A1) every relevant mutation declares its invalidations; (A2) every registered fact has one correct executable verifier owner; (A3) the verifier is sound for the property it checks; and (A4) boundary transitions invoke the scheduler. We do not prove these assumptions or completeness of the fact universe.
