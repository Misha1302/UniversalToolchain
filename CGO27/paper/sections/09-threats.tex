\section{Threats to Validity and Limitations}
\paragraph{Authorship and corpus size.}
The primary, challenge, source-to-result, and TensorRules faults are author/model designed. They demonstrate concrete failures but may align with implementation structure. The external-author protocol is executable, yet no human-authored frozen archive is included; independent-fault prevalence and broad generalization remain unsupported.

\paragraph{Relative rather than absolute safety.}
A missing invalidation declaration can prevent P2 from creating an obligation. P3 is a stronger evaluated oracle only for registered routes; neither policy detects a property for which no verifier exists. The boundary-safety statement is therefore conditional on contract completeness, route uniqueness, scheduler invocation, and verifier soundness.

\paragraph{Baseline scope.}
The demand-driven comparison is a minimal controlled witness, not a full implementation of every production analysis manager. It isolates the case where an invalidated semantic fact has no later consumer query. A larger demand-driven source-to-result corpus would strengthen the comparison.

\paragraph{Generality.}
Two packages are stronger evidence than one, but both execute in one framework and share scheduler concepts. TensorRules differs in syntax and shape/layout invariants, yet it is small and model-authored.

\paragraph{Performance.}
The boundary harness records verifier-kernel timings, and P2 uses fewer calls on clean boundaries. Shared CI is noisy and the reduction is below the frozen threshold. Whole-compilation overhead and speedup remain unknown until a pinned-machine experiment controls governor, affinity, background load, process repetitions, and confidence intervals.

\paragraph{Repair during study.}
The unchanged P07 program revealed mixed numeric promotion missing from native arithmetic. We report the repair and retain the case as a regression control. Historical evidence with the failure remains distinguishable from the new corpus revision; the repair does not affect any fault injection or semantic-verification route.
